Timeline: The first alert fired on the metrics dashboard, followed by customer reports in the support channel. Engineers confirmed the issue, identified the suspect deployment, and coordinated a rollback while monitoring error rates and latency.

Post-incident review: The outage was traced to a misconfigured database connection pool. The pool size was reduced during routine maintenance, which caused contention under normal peak load. The fix involved restoring appropriate pool parameters and adding a pre-deployment check.

Follow-up actions: Add automated checks for configuration drift, improve alert descriptions to reduce ambiguity, and document decision points from the incident to refine the response playbook.
